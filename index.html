Type today's plan on the right. Open "Edit figures" to change any other column. Leave a box empty to use the file value; your changes are outlined in gold.

Plan and "balance to today's plan" are kept for today. Names and targets are kept until you reset. Actual, today billed and "balance to EB" edits clear when you upload a new file, since the new file has fresh figures.

3. Poster details

Campaign

Each scheme remembers its own heading, phasing % and closing date. Switch scheme and they change with it.

Unit and team

Each unit (ASM) keeps its own unit and team name. They appear on the poster everywhere the unit is mentioned.

Logo and Opus boy image

The Birla Opus logo and Opus boy are on every poster by default. In the full design, the Opus boy, taglines and beach scene come from your model poster; an uploaded logo replaces the logo there too. Upload a different image only if you want to replace them.
